Four digits. All different. Your rival has chosen a secret code — can you crack it before they crack yours?

CodeCracker is a head-to-head logic game. Each guess is answered with two numbers: how many digits are in the right place, and how many are right but in the wrong place. The answers never say which digits matched — working that out is the game.

THREE GAMES IN ONE
• CodeCracker — the code-breaking duel at the heart of the app
• Tic-Tac-Toe — the classic, three in a row
• Four in a Row — drop your discs, line up four

THREE WAYS TO PLAY
• Pass & play — two players share one device
• vs Computer — easy, medium or hard; on hard, the computer plays CodeCracker with a solver that rarely needs more than a handful of guesses
• Nearby — two iPhones or iPads play device to device, no internet needed

FAIR PLAY ON TWO DEVICES
Your secret code never leaves your phone. Each side locks in a fingerprint of its code at the start, and both are checked when the game ends — so nobody can quietly change their code halfway through.
